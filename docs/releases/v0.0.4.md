A patch for moving around a zoomed-in photo.

## Fixed

- **Half-drawn frames on macOS.** Dragging a zoomed photo quickly could
  flash a frame with most of the picture missing. The window drew into a
  screen buffer macOS was still showing; now it takes turns between three
  and only draws into one macOS is done with (core9000 0.1.3).
- **Empty edges while dragging.** The edge you drag into used to stay empty
  until the sharp preview caught up. Now a small preview of the whole photo,
  with your steps, sits underneath, so the edge shows the photo softly for
  that moment.

## Tested

A new test drags a zoomed 16-megapixel photo back and forth faster than
previews arrive and checks every frame: none of the photo may be empty, and
drawing only what changed must match drawing everything.
